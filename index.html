Caregiver Guide

Welcome to Mon and the Bāṁshi — an augmented reality picture book about emotions.

1.Open the book to any illustrated page. Hold your phone camera steadily over the page until the animation appears.
2.When a flute button appears at the bottom of the screen, tap it to open the bāṁshi melody for that emotion.
3.Glowing holes will appear one at a time on the flute. Guide your child to tap each glowing hole in order to play the melody.
4.Once the full melody is played, an animation will appear showing what the character is feeling. Take a moment to talk about that emotion together.
5.Use the sound button to play gentle forest sounds throughout the experience.

Each melody is rooted in Bengali musical tradition and is designed to reflect the emotion of each character.
